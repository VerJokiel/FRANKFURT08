    Inside the index

    Breadth

    Share of members trading above their own 50-day and 200-day averages. A rising index with falling breadth means a few large names carry it.

    Average correlation

    Mean of all pairwise correlations between the 40 members' daily returns over three months. High values mean macro news drives everything; diversification inside the index then works less.

    Dispersion

    Spread of members' one-month returns (cross-sectional standard deviation). High dispersion means stock picking matters more than the index direction.

    Sectors

    Data and method

    • Prices: daily closes of the 40 CAC 40 members on Euronext Paris and the index (^FCHI), adjusted for dividends and splits, from Yahoo Finance through the open-source yfinance library. Trading hours 09:00–17:30 Paris time; times on this page are Paris time.
    • Rates and currency: ECB Data Portal API: deposit facility rate (FM), 10-year AAA yield (YC), long-term government bond yields of France and Germany (IRS, monthly) and the EUR/USD reference rate (EXR).
    • Weights: , computed from full market capitalisation with the 15% single-name cap the index applies. The official index uses free-float market caps, so weights are close but not exact.
    • Correlation views: the matrix, network and sector clusters show the 20 largest members by weight, so they stay readable; the average correlation, breadth and market map use all 40 members.
    • Live updates: during trading hours one GitHub Actions job (live-markets.yml) fetches 5-minute bars for all five market pages every 5 minutes and publishes them to the live-markets branch. This page checks for a new file every 30 seconds and recomputes everything on each new bar. Yahoo delays exchange quotes by about 15 minutes and GitHub can start jobs a few minutes late, so live figures trail the market by roughly 15–25 minutes. Real-time prices need a paid data licence.
    • Daily update: after the session the same job runs python/build_market.py (about 18:10 Frankfurt time) and commits the official closes to data/markets/cac40.js; update-markets.yml repeats it later as a backup. If a fetch fails, the last good data stays online.
    • Short-interval correlation: 5-minute returns understate correlation because stocks do not all trade in the same seconds (the Epps effect); the 30-minute and daily windows correct for most of it.
    • Metrics: returns use closes 1, 5, 21 and 252 sessions back. VaR and ES are historical, from the last 252 daily index returns. Average correlation uses the identity Σρij = Var(Σzi) for standardised returns. A new 52-week high means the highest close of the last 10 sessions beats the previous 242.
    • Members: Euronext's Index Steering Committee reviews the CAC 40 quarterly (March, June, September, December). The list in python/markets.py needs updating after each review.

    Data: Yahoo Finance (via yfinance), the ECB Data Portal. Intraday prices are 5-minute bars delayed about 15 minutes by the source; nothing on this page is real-time. Index weights are approximations from market capitalisation.
