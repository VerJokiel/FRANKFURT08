    The index

    Daily close in index points with its 200-day average. Trading above a rising 200-day average is the usual definition of an uptrend.

    Distance below the highest close so far in the window.

    Realised volatility

    Standard deviation of the last 21 daily returns, annualised, against its one-year average.

    Inside the index

    Breadth

    Share of members trading above their own 50-day and 200-day averages. A rising index with falling breadth means a few large names carry it.

    Average correlation

    Mean of all pairwise correlations between the 20 members' daily returns over three months. High values mean macro news drives everything; diversification inside the index then works less.

    Dispersion

    Spread of members' one-month returns (cross-sectional standard deviation). High dispersion means stock picking matters more than the index direction.

    Sectors

    Members grouped by sector and weighted by index weight. Volatility and the average correlation inside the sector use the last year of daily returns.

    Rates and currency

    Interest rates

    NBP reference rate (Bank for International Settlements policy-rate data) and the 10-year Polish government bond yield (monthly average, ECB long-term interest rate statistics).

    Złoty

    ECB reference rates: złoty per euro and per US dollar (the dollar rate is derived from the two euro rates). A weaker złoty raises the złoty value of exporters' sales, such as KGHM's copper.

    Data and method

    • Prices: daily closes of the 20 WIG20 members on Warsaw Stock Exchange (GPW) and the index (WIG20.WA), adjusted for dividends and splits, from Yahoo Finance through the open-source yfinance library. The index has no daily history on Yahoo, so its closes before today are the last hourly bar of each session (they match the official close). Trading hours 09:00–17:00 Warsaw time; times on this page are Warsaw time.
    • Rates and currency: BIS central bank policy rates (WS_CBPOL) for the NBP reference rate; ECB Data Portal: 10-year Polish yield (IRS, monthly) and the EUR/PLN and EUR/USD reference rates (EXR).
    • Weights: , computed from full market capitalisation with the 15% single-name cap the index applies. The official index uses free-float market caps, so weights are close but not exact.
    • Correlation views: the matrix, network and sector clusters show the 20 largest members by weight, so they stay readable; the average correlation, breadth and market map use all 20 members.
    • Live updates: during trading hours one GitHub Actions job (live-markets.yml) fetches 5-minute bars for all five market pages every 5 minutes and publishes them to the live-markets branch. This page checks for a new file every 30 seconds and recomputes everything on each new bar. Yahoo delays exchange quotes by about 15 minutes and GitHub can start jobs a few minutes late, so live figures trail the market by roughly 15–25 minutes. Real-time prices need a paid data licence.
    • Daily update: after the session the same job runs python/build_market.py (about 18:10 Frankfurt time) and commits the official closes to data/markets/wig20.js; update-markets.yml repeats it later as a backup. If a fetch fails, the last good data stays online.
    • Short-interval correlation: 5-minute returns understate correlation because stocks do not all trade in the same seconds (the Epps effect); the 30-minute and daily windows correct for most of it.
    • Metrics: returns use closes 1, 5, 21 and 252 sessions back. VaR and ES are historical, from the last 252 daily index returns. Average correlation uses the identity Σρij = Var(Σzi) for standardised returns. A new 52-week high means the highest close of the last 10 sessions beats the previous 242.
    • Members: GPW Benchmark revises the WIG20 annually in March and adjusts it quarterly (June, September, December). The list in python/markets.py needs updating after each review.

    Data: Yahoo Finance (via yfinance), the ECB Data Portal, the Bank for International Settlements. Intraday prices are 5-minute bars delayed about 15 minutes by the source; nothing on this page is real-time. Index weights are approximations from market capitalisation.
