Data and method

  • Data: the same files as the index pages: daily closes (Yahoo Finance via yfinance, adjusted for dividends and splits) in data/dax40.js and data/markets/<id>.js, refreshed after each session; 5-minute bars from the live-data (DAX) and live-markets branches, delayed about 15 minutes by the source.
  • Today: while an exchange is open, its index and members get a provisional price for today (last close × latest 5-minute price ÷ previous close), and every figure for that index includes it. Closed exchanges show their last close.
  • Currencies: every index is measured in its own currency. For a euro investor the WIG20 and the FTSE 100 also carry the złoty and pound exchange rate, which the index pages show.
  • Members: current members only (survivorship); weights are approximations from market capitalisation. Each index page explains its own data in detail.

Data: Yahoo Finance (via yfinance). Intraday prices are 5-minute bars delayed about 15 minutes by the source; nothing on this page is real-time.
